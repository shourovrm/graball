THE SMALL PRINT, SIMPLIFIED

A few things
worth knowing.

Can I download from every website?+

Graball uses yt-dlp to support many websites. Some sites restrict downloads, change how videos are delivered, or require login. If a link doesn’t work, we’ll tell you.

Where does my video go?+

Once the file is ready, select “Save video to device.” Your browser saves it to your downloads folder or asks where to put it. Temporary server files expire after one hour.

Why does preparing a video take time?+

Some websites store video and audio separately. Graball downloads both and combines them before your file is ready. Longer videos and higher resolutions take more time.

What am I allowed to download?+

Download videos you own or have permission to save, and follow the source website’s terms. Graball does not bypass DRM or account restrictions.
